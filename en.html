Primary Crops & Operations

Farm-Grown Strawberries

Cultivated on raised plastic-mulch ridges with elite Dutch seedlings (De Kemp). Drip irrigation, mineral-rich soil, and superb shelf-life for wholesale supply.

Greenhouse Vegetables

2,000 m² covered growing capacity. Early sweet corn under plastic tunnels, as well as premium bell and Kapia peppers direct from the grower.

Arable Crop Farming

Cultivation of milling wheat, feed corn, sunflower, barley, and soy on 120 hectares with modern machinery and diligent agronomic care.
