AGRO FEHYR: Strawberry Cultivation & Grain Export in Matiyovo

For the Fehyr family, the village of Matiyovo (Berehove district, Transcarpathia) and its arable lands have been our daily work and vocation for a quarter of a century. Building on this solid agricultural expertise, we established our farming enterprise. The core of our business is AGRO FEHYR: Strawberry Cultivation & Grain Export in Matiyovo. On our 120-hectare territory, we use modern agrotechnics to turn the characteristics of the heavy, compact soil, which is naturally rich in potassium, to our advantage. We cultivate our crops in the warmest climate region of Transcarpathia, on ideal fields with a neutral pH level, which is why our strawberries begin to ripen much earlier, and thanks to these natural minerals, this area yields the sweetest strawberries and corn. Our main profile is the large-scale wholesale of premium quality fresh strawberries at producer prices, and serving large-scale, high-quality maritime grain export logistics.

Our Cultivation: Strawberries, Vegetables & Large-Scale Grain Export

Large-Scale Farm-Grown Strawberries

Large-scale wholesale distribution directly from the grower. In the strawberry fields of Matiyovo, our strawberries are not cultivated in open fields but exclusively on raised ridges (plastic mulch beds), utilizing elite Dutch seedlings (De Kemp B.V.). Deep well irrigation, combined with the soil's naturally high calcium and potassium content, guarantee our wholesale partners an excellent shelf-life, uniform shape, and intense flavor profile for the premium market.

Intensive Greenhouse Vegetable Cultivation

2,000 m² of covered greenhouse capacity on our farm in Matiyovo. We engage in weather-independent high-value vegetable cultivation, focusing on growing greenhouse corn under plastic mulch, while our tunnels also successfully produce premium bell and Kapia peppers in bulk.

Large-scale Ukraine Grain Farming & Export

Large-scale arable farming of export-grade milling wheat, feed corn, sunflower, barley, and soy on the resilient soils of Matiyovo. Thanks to precision agrotechnics, modern agricultural machinery, and proper tillage, our farm-grown grain yields meet the strictest moisture and protein requirements. We provide comprehensive logistics for cleaned quality grains, making us a trusted producer in international maritime grain export.

Modern Agrotechnics and Reliable B2B Logistics

Our farm strives for maximum quality not only in cultivation but also in post-harvest processes. Through precision tillage, a strong agricultural machinery fleet, and rational nutrient management, our export-grade milling wheat and feed corn perfectly meet expectations. Following the harvest, grain drying and bulk cargo logistics are strictly supervised by us, ensuring the safe maritime export of quality grains to global markets.

For our premium strawberries and greenhouse vegetables, preserving freshness is paramount. Packaging into containers and dispatch of the goods take place directly from the fields. Our dispatch point in Matiyovo is easily accessible for heavy goods vehicles, making international freight transport and loading fast and seamless.
